# Motiflow Project Charter

## Vision
Build the operating system for enterprise creative intelligence, transforming business intent into governed, explainable creative execution.

## Mission
Enable organizations to move beyond prompt engineering by orchestrating specialized AI engines through a trustworthy workflow.

## North Star
Every creative decision should be traceable, measurable, reusable, and aligned with business outcomes.

## Guiding Principles
- Understand before generating.
- Reason before rendering.
- Meaning over aesthetics.
- Human oversight for high-impact decisions.
- Model-agnostic architecture.
- Canonical contracts between engines.
- Quality through orchestration.

## Product Pillars
1. Creative Intelligence
2. Workflow Orchestration
3. Enterprise Governance
4. Knowledge & Memory
5. Multi-model Execution

## Strategic Goal
Evolve Motiflow from a creative application into a platform with SDKs, connectors, governance, and an ecosystem for enterprise creative workflows.
