# Motiflow AI Collaborator Context

Motiflow is an enterprise creative-intelligence platform. Its underlying architecture is ACDS: the Autonomous Creative Direction System.

## Product intent

Transform business meaning, research, audience context, and brand constraints into explainable creative direction and production-ready assets through governed workflows.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Understand before generating.
- Separate reasoning from rendering.
- Use explicit, versioned artifact contracts.
- Preserve provenance and human edits.
- Prefer one coherent narrative and metaphor before variation.
- Keep provider dependencies behind adapters.
- Use human approval at consequential gates.
- Build the smallest complete workflow first.

## Current phase

Foundation and architecture definition. Production code has not yet become the source of truth.

## Canonical reading order

The canonical reading order is defined in `/START_HERE.md`: `START_HERE.md` → `PROJECT_CHARTER.md` → `MASTER_CONTEXT.md` → `CONTEXT_INDEX.yaml` → `MEOS/20_PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md`. Do not maintain a competing order here.

## Collaboration rule

Before proposing implementation, identify the relevant canonical artifact, contract, ADR, and success criterion. Do not silently invent product behavior that conflicts with documented principles. Record unresolved decisions rather than disguising assumptions as settled architecture.
