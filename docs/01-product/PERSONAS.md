# Product Personas

## 1. Creative Lead

Owns creative quality and direction across campaigns or content programs.

**Needs:** faster concept development, coherent direction, review visibility, controlled variation, and clear rationale.

**Pain points:** fragmented briefs, repeated rework, inconsistent outputs, hidden prompt logic, and weak traceability.

## 2. Brand and Marketing Manager

Owns message consistency, campaign outcomes, and brand governance.

**Needs:** alignment to audience and business goals, brand-safe outputs, approvals, and measurable performance.

**Pain points:** beautiful work that misses the strategy, slow feedback loops, and difficulty enforcing brand rules.

## 3. Creative Strategist

Transforms research and business context into narratives, concepts, and visual territories.

**Needs:** structured research synthesis, metaphor exploration, evidence, alternative comparison, and reusable knowledge.

**Pain points:** scattered source material, shallow AI ideation, and loss of reasoning between stages.

## 4. Designer or Content Producer

Turns approved direction into production assets.

**Needs:** clear creative packages, constraints, prompt specifications, references, variants, and actionable feedback.

**Pain points:** ambiguous requests, contradictory feedback, and late strategic changes.

## 5. Enterprise Administrator

Owns access, policy, integrations, security, and operational oversight.

**Needs:** identity controls, audit logs, data boundaries, connector governance, reliability, and cost visibility.

**Pain points:** unmanaged AI usage, provider lock-in, unclear data handling, and limited observability.

## 6. Platform Developer

Builds custom engines, connectors, workflows, and product extensions.

**Needs:** stable contracts, SDKs, test harnesses, event schemas, examples, and versioning rules.

**Pain points:** tightly coupled prompts, undocumented state, inconsistent payloads, and brittle orchestration.
